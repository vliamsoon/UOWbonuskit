⚖️ The Golden Rules of Academic AI

Technology changes rapidly, but academic integrity remains. This final section outlines the three non-negotiable principles for using AI safely and effectively at UOW Malaysia. Keep these rules in mind as you deploy the tools and prompts from the sections above.

"AI is your assistant, not your replacement. You are the expert; AI is the engine."

🔒

Data Privacy is Paramount

Never upload student names, ID numbers, raw grades, or sensitive, unpublished university data into public AI tools. Always anonymize data (e.g., use "Student A") before pasting it into any prompt.

📝

Grade the Edit, Not the Draft

Since AI can write a basic essay in seconds, shift your assessments. Have students use AI to generate a baseline draft, then grade them on their ability to critique, correct, and elevate that draft.

✅

Trust, but Verify

Always check the output. AI is a reasoning engine, not a factual database. Use NotebookLM for literature analysis to keep the AI grounded in verified facts, and always verify citations.
